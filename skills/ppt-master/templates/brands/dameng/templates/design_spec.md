## III. Typography

| Role | Family | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| title | `"Microsoft YaHei", "微软雅黑", "PingFang SC", Arial, sans-serif` | 700 |
| body | `"Microsoft YaHei", "微软雅黑", "PingFang SC", Arial, sans-serif` | 400 |
| data | `Arial, "Microsoft YaHei", sans-serif` | 600-700 |

中文字体优先使用现代无衬线中文字体 `Microsoft YaHei` 与 `PingFang SC`；关键数据指标推荐采用等宽数字字体 `DIN` 或 `Arial`。

## IV. Logo

| File | Form | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| `../images/logo.png` | 达梦官方彩色 Logo Lockup (850x134) | 封面、封底左上角品牌背书，以及内容页右上角品牌标识 |

- Cover / ending: use the full lockup at a readable size.
- Content pages: use the lockup only when the title area has sufficient space; avoid repeating it as oversized decoration.
- Keep clearspace around the lockup and avoid busy image backgrounds.
- On dark backgrounds, use an owner-approved reverse asset when supplied; otherwise place this logo on a clean white backing plate.
- Do not substitute product or event assets for the corporate lockup unless the presentation scope explicitly calls for that identity.
- External publication must follow the owner's trademark and asset permissions; packaging the logo does not grant usage rights.

## V. Voice & Tone

- Formality: formal-authoritative
- Person: organization-first, "达梦数据 / 我们"
- Emoji: avoid
- Abbreviations: spell out database and system names on first use; retain standard technical terms such as RDBMS, SQL, OLTP when context is clear
- Style: emphasize core-system reliability, self-developed engine security, customer value, national benchmark proofs, and measurable performance

## VI. Icon Style

- Preference: clean stroke or solid geometric icons
- Recommended libraries: `tabler-outline`, `lucide`, or one consistent filled family when higher visual weight is needed
- Metaphors: prefer database, server, high-availability cluster, transaction, security shield, enterprise core, and industry-benchmark metaphors
